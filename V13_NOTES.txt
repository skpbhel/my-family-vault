My Family Vault – Structured Records v13
Education / Coaching Test Performance

Major Test:
- JEE Main: 1 paper, 300 marks; 100 marks and 25 questions per subject.
- JEE Advanced: 2 papers; each paper 180 marks; 60 marks and 17 questions per subject.
- Combined Major maximum: 660 marks.

Minor Test:
- One paper only.
- Select JEE Main pattern or JEE Advanced pattern.

Recorded per subject and paper:
Correct, Wrong, Unattempted, Marks scored.
The app validates the question count and automatically calculates paper and overall totals.

Also recorded:
Rank, Total students, Test date, Test name/number, Coaching institute, Notes.

Analysis:
Score %, Accuracy %, Attempt rate, C/W/U, relative cohort position, subject performance,
strongest subject, review-priority subject, highest wrong rate, highest unattempted rate,
trend charts, latest paper breakdown and next expected test date based on a 21-day cycle.

Advanced marks are entered from the official scorecard because Advanced marking schemes may vary.
